# AVENIQ — 90-second walkthrough

**0–15s:** “Most CRMs tell us what happened. AVENIQ helps revenue leaders decide what to do next. This is our live synthetic CRM workspace with 500 linked customer accounts and 750 opportunities.”

**15–30s:** “Here is the revenue command center. ARR, weighted pipeline and at-risk exposure are calculated from the dataset—not pasted into dashboard cards. We can filter by enterprise, mid-market or SMB.”

**30–45s:** “Let's open an at-risk customer. The health score is explainable: product adoption, customer engagement, support issues and upcoming renewal each contribute points. This is a transparent rule-based signal, not an invented churn probability.”

**45–60s:** “AVENIQ creates actions with evidence and suggested owners. We can accept or dismiss recommendations, with local tracking in the demo.”

**60–75s:** “In the Revenue War Room, Sales, Marketing, and Customer Success review the same record from different angles. A coordinated plan reconciles their priorities. Today these are deterministic perspectives; LLM orchestration is a roadmap item.”

**75–90s:** “Ask AVENIQ directly which deals are stalled, or run a scenario to see how changing conversion and retention assumptions affects the opportunity. This prototype demonstrates how CRM data becomes transparent, actionable decision intelligence.”
